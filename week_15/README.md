# Week 15 – Logic Gates Interactive Modules

## Focus
- Complete the interactive modules on basic and composite logic gates at https://openlab.citytech.cuny.edu/dotscst1100/modules/the-great-gates-and-their-circuit-circus/
- Refresh truth tables, gate symbols, and how gates combine in small circuits (AND/OR/NOT, plus NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR).

## What to Do
- Work through each section of the online module in order; watch demos and try every interactive prompt.
- Build or check the truth tables when asked; pause the videos to predict outputs before revealing answers.
- Take brief notes on at least two real-world scenarios from the module (e.g., alarms, toggles, adders) and which gates they use.

## Deliverable (Due Next Class)
- 1–2 paragraph reflection (150–250 words) summarizing:
  - One gate you felt most confident with and why.
  - One gate or concept that needs more practice (include a quick question you still have).
  - A screenshot or short description of one interactive you completed.
- Format: PDF or Markdown; upload to the LMS before class.

## Tips
- Keep a scrap sheet nearby to sketch inputs/outputs before you click “check.”
- If a table feels tricky, rewrite inputs as plain language (e.g., `A = password correct`, `B = code correct`).
- Stuck on a prompt? Revisit the earlier “Great Gates” explainer sections before moving forward.
