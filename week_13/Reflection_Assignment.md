# Week 13 Reflection: Application Layer in Practice

**Length:** 200–300 words. **Format:** PDF or Markdown. **Due:** Next class (Week 14).

## Prompts (answer all)
1) Pick ONE scenario you care about (examples: campus club events, tutoring center, gear checkout, small shop online orders). In 2–3 sentences, describe the raw data you’d collect and the information you need from it (reports, alerts, decisions).
2) Tools: Would you start with a spreadsheet or a database? Explain why using one design habit from the reading (e.g., ranges instead of hard-coded counts, keys to avoid duplicates, avoiding circular references, constraints for valid values).
3) Data quality & security: Name one thing that could go wrong (price mismatch, duplicate records, missing WHERE in UPDATE/DELETE, bad key, phishing/cart tampering) and one safeguard (constraints, HTTPS, tokenization/payment processor, audit trail, clear IDs).
4) After reading: What is one design change you’d make to your scenario next time (formula tweak, key choice, relationship/cardinality fix, better query) and why?

## Grading (12 points)
- **Connection (4 pts):** Scenario ties to application-layer ideas (spreadsheets, DB/SQL, or e-commerce flow).
- **Design reasoning (4 pts):** Clear tool choice with a specific habit from the reading (ranges vs constants, keys, constraints, avoiding circular refs).
- **Risk & safeguard (2 pts):** Names a plausible failure and a matching protection.
- **Clarity & length (2 pts):** 200–300 words, readable, on time.
