# Week 14 - Research Paper Drafting Workshop (Intro + First Body Paragraph)

Focus: Extend the Week 12 research launch by moving from a chosen topic to drafting the opening paragraphs of Formal Writing Assignment #1. Class time is split between a guided writing sprint and a mid-point peer review so every student leaves with an intro, a first body paragraph, and feedback.

## Learning Objectives
- Draft an engaging introduction that frames the problem, stakes, and a clear working thesis.
- Write a first body paragraph with a focused topic sentence, evidence from at least one credible source, and correct MLA in-text citation.
- Integrate quotations or paraphrases smoothly with analysis that connects back to the thesis.
- Exchange constructive peer feedback on focus, clarity, and citation mechanics.

## Materials
- Laptop with Google Docs or Microsoft Word set up in MLA format.
- Your approved/working research topic and notes from the Week 12 freewrite.
- At least two credible sources open (PDFs, database links, or annotated notes).
- Purdue OWL MLA Formatting and Style Guide.
- Activity guide: `drafting_first_paragraphs_workshop.md`.

## Suggested Agenda (90 minutes)
1. **Warm-up focus (10 min)**  
   - Individually restate your topic and working claim; note any scope issues to fix during drafting.
2. **Mini-lesson: paragraph moves (10 min)**  
   - Model an intro’s three moves (hook/context → significance → thesis) and a body paragraph skeleton (topic sentence → evidence with citation → analysis → link).
3. **Writing sprint #1 — Introduction (20 min)**  
   - Draft the intro paragraph; highlight/underline the thesis and flag any gaps in background info.
4. **Peer review + discussion (20 min)**  
   - Swap intros in pairs (or trios if uneven), use the checklist to comment on clarity, stakes, and MLA citation setup; surface common issues as a group.
5. **Writing sprint #2 — First body paragraph (25 min)**  
   - Compose a paragraph that integrates at least one source (quote or paraphrase) with MLA citation and analysis tied to your thesis.
6. **Exit ticket + next steps (5 min)**  
   - Note one revision you will make tonight and one source you need to locate; upload your doc to the LMS.

## Deliverables & Follow-Up
- By end of class: One intro paragraph + one body paragraph in MLA format with a highlighted thesis and at least one in-text citation.
- Peer review comment(s) attached or shared (track changes/comments in Docs/Word).
- Homework: Revise using feedback, draft a second body paragraph, add a provisional Works Cited page for sources cited so far, and finish an initial full draft to share next week.
