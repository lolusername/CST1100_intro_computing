## Week 12 - MLA Formatting & Research Paper Launch

Focus: Teach MLA format to beginners using Purdue OWL guidance and guide students as they choose a topic for Formal Writing Assignment #1 (see `updated_course_materials/4 Required Formal Writing Assignments scaffolding 10_20.pdf`).

### Learning Objectives
- Identify the purpose of MLA style and describe key differences from other formats.
- Configure a blank document so it meets MLA rules for margins, spacing, fonts, headers, and headings.
- Produce correct in-text citations and Works Cited entries for books, scholarly articles, and credible web/media sources.
- Evaluate and narrow research ideas into arguable topics supported by preliminary sources.

### Materials
- Slide deck: `mla_formatting_and_research_week12.pptx`
- In-class activity: `mla_in_class_exercise.md`
- Research topic freewrite prompt: `research_topic_freewrite.md`
- Purdue OWL MLA Formatting and Style Guide (https://owl.purdue.edu/)
- Research assignment overview: `updated_course_materials/4 Required Formal Writing Assignments scaffolding 10_20.pdf`

### Suggested Agenda
1. **Hook & context**
   - Share how MLA keeps academic writing consistent; preview the upcoming research paper.
2. **Direct instruction: MLA basics**
   - Use the deck to model document setup, headings, and sample in-text citations.
3. **Modeling Works Cited entries**
   - Walk through Purdue OWL templates for books, journal articles, and websites.
4. **Guided practice**
   - Students replicate document setup on their laptops with live support.
5. **In-class MLA exercise**
   - Students complete the scavenger hunt, paragraph, and Works Cited tasks described in `mla_in_class_exercise.md`.
6. **Research topic freewrite**
   - Use `research_topic_freewrite.md` to guide a 250-word reflection on topic choice, relevance, and anticipated sources.
7. **Share-out & exit ticket**
   - Students submit freewrite plus 2-3 tentative topics and one MLA skill they want feedback on.

### Deliverables & Follow-Up
- MLA practice document exported as PDF or DOCX for instructor review.
- Research topic freewrite (250+ words) uploaded the same day.
- List of tentative research topics with at least two supporting sources each (due Week 13 check-in).
- Continued use of Purdue OWL as the reference for all Formal Writing Assignments.
