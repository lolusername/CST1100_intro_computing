# Week 12 Research Topic Freewrite

## Purpose
Jump-start the research paper process by clarifying what you want to investigate, why the topic matters to you or your community, and what kinds of sources you will need. This freewrite is informal but must be thoughtful and specific. Minimum 250 words.

## Prompt
Write in paragraph form (stream-of-consciousness is fine) responding to the questions below. You may type directly in Word/Google Docs or in a notebook, but submit a typed version before you leave class.

1. **Topic focus**: What issue, question, or controversy do you want to explore for Formal Writing Assignment #1? Describe it in your own words.
2. **Personal/academic relevance**: Why does this topic matter to you, your community, or your future goals? What makes it interesting enough to research for several weeks?
3. **Working claim or angle**: What early stance, hypothesis, or guiding question do you have? (It can change later.)
4. **Anticipated sources**: List at least three types of sources you expect to consult. Be specific--for example, "peer-reviewed education journals," "NYC government open data," "interviews with student club leaders," "tech equity think tank reports." Explain what information you hope each type of source will provide.
5. **Research challenges**: Identify at least one obstacle you expect (limited data, paywalled articles, biased coverage, etc.) and how you plan to address it.

## Submission Requirements
- 250+ words, typed, double-spaced, standard font (match MLA setup if possible).
- Include your name, course, date at the top of the page.
- Save as `Lastname_Firstname_Week12_Freewrite.docx` or `.pdf` and upload to the LMS before class ends.

## Quick Checklist
- Did you clearly name the topic and describe its scope?
- Did you explain why the topic matters to you and/or the course outcomes?
- Did you list multiple source types and their intended use?
- Did you anticipate at least one research challenge with a strategy?
- Is your document saved and uploaded?

## Instructor Use
This freewrite helps the instructor:
- Provide early feedback on topic focus and feasibility.
- Recommend databases, keywords, and experts.
- Spot potential roadblocks before you dive into formal research.
